Publications Working Papers Teaching Contact

Hi! I am a Tenured Associate Professor and Ph.D. Supervisor at the School of Computer Science and Technology, Beijing Institute of Technology. I received my Ph.D. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, under the supervision of Prof. Xiaotie Deng, and completed my B.S. in Computer Science at SJTU's ACM Class.

I am interested in theoretical computer science. Currently, I mainly work on algorithmic game theory and computational complexity theory.

I am always looking for self-motivated undergraduate, Master's, and Ph.D. students who share an interest in theoretical research. If you are interested in working together, please feel free to get in touch!
