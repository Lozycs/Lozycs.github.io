Zhengyang Liu (刘正阳)

Publications Working Papers Teaching Contact

Hi! I am an Associate Professor at the School of Computer Science and Technology, Beijing Institute of Technology. I received my Ph.D. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, under the supervision of Prof. Xiaotie Deng, and completed my B.S. in Computer Science at SJTU's ACM Honor Class.

I am interested in algorithmic game theory and complexity theory.

Prospective Students: I am actively looking for self-motivated undergraduate, Master's, and Ph.D. students interested in theoretical computer science. If you would like to work together, please feel free to reach out!
